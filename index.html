HashFarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game HashFarm (package name com.hashfarm.game, the "Game") handles information. The Game is published by Pocket Yeti Games LLC ("we", "us"). HashFarm is a simulation: it uses fictional coins and has no real cryptocurrency and no cash-out.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Your game progress (cash, upgrades, settings and similar) is saved only on your device. We do not receive a copy of it. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. We do not control this collection. You can learn more in Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Consent for players in the EEA, UK and similar regions

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the Game shows a consent message before personalized ads are used. You can accept, decline, or change your choice later from the options provided in the Game when available, or by clearing the Game's data to see the message again.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional purchases (such as gem packs and an ad-free option). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the Game which purchases you own so they can be delivered and restored.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we hold no personal data about you on our servers.

7. Your choices and data deletion

Because progress is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in Android settings. For questions or requests about data handled by Google, see the Google links above or contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
